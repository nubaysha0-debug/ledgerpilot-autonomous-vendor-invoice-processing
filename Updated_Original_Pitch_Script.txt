GOVERNANCE GAP
3. Absence of Approval Controls
Accountants record and pay bills without structured role separation, cryptographic approval tokens, or audit logging.
Impact: Severe fraud risk, unverified remittance changes, and zero accountability trail.

ROLE SEGREGATION (MAKER vs CHECKER)
1. Accountant Role (Sunita — Maker):
• Reviews incoming invoices and records payment requests.
• Prepares draft references (e.g. ₹2,500 NEFT request against ABC Electricals).
• Absolutely CANNOT approve or execute transfers.

2. Business Owner Role (Ramesh — Checker):
• Inspects pending requests in dedicated approval queue.
• Authorizes or rejects payment requests with mandatory reason tracking.
• Triggers simulated NEFT/UPI execution and retains sole control over money movement.

DYNAMIC APPROVAL FINGERPRINTS & THE LIVE MIC-DROP
• Cryptographic Fingerprinting: Approval keys hash the exact invoice total, invoice version, vendor ID, and bank account details.
• Automatic Stale Invalidation: On Prime Solar Solutions (PSS/26-27/0121), when a supplier slips in a ₹500 revised freight charge post-approval, the approval immediately turns STALE.
• Re-Approval Enforcement: The 'Execute' button physically vanishes and is replaced with 'Re-approve', blocking tampered payouts.
• Idempotent Retries: Stored Idempotency-Key guarantees that network retries or 10 parallel execute calls result in exactly 1 debit and 9 safe replays (Acceptance Test T20).
